Wi-Fi QR Display

A dedicated touchscreen that makes joining a changing guest network quick, visible and easy.

From a QR code to a connected display

The project began as a simple way to display guest Wi-Fi credentials. It grew into a Raspberry Pi 4 and official seven-inch touchscreen that rotates background imagery, refreshes its QR code and sends updated credentials through Telegram.

A visible text password is also available for devices that cannot scan QR codes. The result is deliberately simple: a compact appliance that solves one household problem reliably.

What it does

  • Presents guest credentials as both QR code and text.
  • Refreshes credentials and artwork automatically.
  • Sends notification messages when access details change.
  • Runs in a self-contained touchscreen enclosure.
