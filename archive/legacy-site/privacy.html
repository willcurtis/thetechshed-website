Privacy notice

This notice explains the limited information processed when you use The Tech Shed.

Contact

If you email hello@thetechshed.dev, your address and message are used only to respond and maintain the resulting correspondence. You can request access, correction or deletion by emailing the same address.

Optional analytics

Plausible Analytics is loaded only after you select “Allow analytics”. Plausible provides aggregated usage statistics without using cookies or creating cross-site profiles. Your choice is stored locally in your browser under analytics-consent.

To reset your choice, clear this site’s local storage in your browser. The site does not use advertising or behavioural profiling.

Configuration generator uploads

CSV and template files submitted to the configuration generator are processed to create the requested download. They are held in a temporary working directory for the duration of the request and are not intentionally retained afterward. Do not upload secrets or personal information.

Server logs and third-party links

The hosting provider may retain standard security and access logs, including IP address, browser details and request time. Links to other websites are governed by those sites’ privacy notices.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal information, including access, correction, deletion, restriction and objection. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority; in the UK this is the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised date.
